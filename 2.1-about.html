About Us

Yuba Coffee Cafe is a popular cafe at Integral University. It is a convenient place for students, faculty
members and visitors to enjoy delicious food and refreshing beverages at affordable prices.

The cafe offers a wide variety of items including hot coffee, tea, cold beverages, shakes, burgers,
sandwiches, momos, noodles, Maggie, pasta, rice, biryani and soups.

Yuba Coffee Cafe is a friendly place where students can relax, enjoy tasty food and spend time with their friends.

What We Offer

  • ☕ Fresh Coffee and Tea
  • 🥤 Refreshing Cold Beverages
  • 🍔 Burgers and Sandwiches
  • 🥟 Momos and Spring Rolls
  • 🍝 Maggie, Noodles and Pasta
  • 🍲 Different varieties of Soups

Our Aim

Our aim is to provide tasty food, refreshing beverages and a comfortable place for students and visitors
at reasonable prices.
